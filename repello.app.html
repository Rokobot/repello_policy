The Growing Threat of Vector-Borne Diseases

Global warming and urbanization have triggered a significant surge in vector-borne diseases and animal conflicts. Pests and wild intruders carry critical viruses and parasites that put family health at risk.

Chemical repellents contain toxic agents like DEET which pollute the ecosystem and cause skin irritation. Repello provides a clean, eco-friendly frequency shield to ward off carriers cleanly.

Mosquito & Tick-Borne Pathogens

Mosquitoes transmit deadly malaria, Dengue, and Zika. Meanwhile, Ticks carry Lyme Disease and Tick-Borne Encephalitis. Repello's Hz generator disrupts their host-seeking behavior.

Rabies & Zoonotic Risks

Stray Dogs, Wolves, Foxes, and Jackals can carry Rabies, a fatal neurotropic virus. Protecting boundaries with ultrasonic barriers prevents aggressive encounters.

Bacterial Contamination

Nesting Rats spread Leptospirosis and Hantavirus via feed contamination, while Moles and Ants ruin backyard ecosystems and introduce soil-borne pathogens.
