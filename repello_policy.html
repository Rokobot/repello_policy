Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

At Repello ("we", "our", "the App"), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information processed through our application. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle device permissions, third-party services, and user data.

1. Information Collection & Permissions

To provide its core features and operate effectively, Repello requires the following permissions and access to your device's features:

  • Camera Permission (AI Scanner): Used solely to take photos of animal or insect bite/scratch marks for classification. The captured images are processed dynamically and are not stored permanently on any server or shared with third parties.

We use Firebase Vertex AI / Gemini API to analyze bite and scratch mark images. The images you scan are securely processed in real-time for classification purposes only. We do not gather or link personal identity details with these scans.

We implement industry-standard security measures to safeguard your technical information. Image files and location inputs are treated dynamically and are not retained in any databases once the session or processing ends.

Repello does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our records.
